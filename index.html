The real mandate

This is not an AI ideas role. It is a transformation operating-system role.

SCIEX needs a leader who can combine DBS discipline with enterprise AI capability, then carry each initiative from business problem to adoption, value realization and sustainment. The work sits across Danaher platforms, SCIEX functions, IT, vendors and frontline workflows.

The success test is not how many pilots launch. It is whether a smaller number of use cases become trusted, measurable and maintainable parts of how the business operates.

Operating contradictions

Five tensions the role must hold at once

  1. SpeedMove quickly enough to learnwithout bypassing quality, authority or risk.
  2. ScaleUse Danaher enterprise capabilitywithout forcing generic solutions into SCIEX-specific work.
  3. ValueBuild an executive portfoliowithout losing the baseline, owner or workflow outcome.
  4. AdoptionDeploy technical capabilitywithout treating training and behavior change as downstream work.
  5. SustainmentCreate momentum through pilotswithout leaving orphaned tools, unclear ownership or recurring support debt.

The hard objection

Russell has not spent his career inside a mass-spectrometry or diagnostics operating company.

That gap should not be disguised. The adjacent case is high-reliability engineering, quality systems, regulated autonomy, global technology translation, Lean/Gemba operating discipline and current AI-first transformation across real business workflows.

The right proof is not a claim of domain equivalence. It is a bounded first workflow, explicit expert authority and measurable evidence that the deployment mechanism works inside SCIEX.
