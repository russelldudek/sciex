Dear Ms. Foster,

SCIEX is not hiring someone merely to generate AI ideas. The Senior Director mandate is to convert enterprise demand into a governed portfolio of adopted workflows - with DBS rigor, clear business ownership, disciplined deployment and evidence that value is real. That is the work I have been building toward across high-reliability engineering, scaled operations and current AI-first operating transformation.

At Vape-Jet, I lead cross-functional operating transformation across production, purchasing, quality, customer success, support, engineering issue flow, ERP/Odoo, Jira, HubSpot, Slack and machine telemetry. At DudeWorth, I develop AI-readiness and workflow frameworks that make value, feasibility, data readiness, governance, human authority, adoption burden and reuse explicit. Earlier, I led Amazon Prime Pittsburgh launch and execution in a 24/7 customer-backward environment and directed engineering, manufacturing and quality systems at Compunetics.

The common thread is practical deployment: make the work visible, define the decision, build the mechanism, establish authority, instrument the outcome and transfer ownership. My candidate vision applies that approach through an AI Deployment Assay - a DBS-compatible method for testing whether a use case has a clear business signal, workable method, control model, measurable response and sustainment owner before it earns scale.

I would enter SCIEX with humility about the domain boundary. I do not claim mass-spectrometry or diagnostics-company tenure. I do bring adjacent evidence in high-reliability technical operations, regulated autonomous systems, quality and process controls, Lean/Gemba leadership and AI-enabled workflow design. I would prove fit through a bounded, expert-owned workflow rather than through inflated equivalence.

The questions I would want to explore are concrete: Where is enterprise AI demand currently outpacing governance capacity? Which two or three workflows would create the strongest cross-functional proof? What decision rights must remain with quality, regulatory, technical or commercial experts? And what evidence would make the steering committee confident enough to standardize, sustain or stop an initiative?

I would welcome the opportunity to discuss how I could help SCIEX make AI measurable enough to scale - and usable enough to stay.

Carpe diem,

Russell Dudek
