## Logo / wordmark decision

The current official site visibly presents the SCIEX identity, but the connected retrieval path did not expose a downloadable, direct official logo or wordmark asset URL. Public search returned third-party copies only. To avoid tracing, recreating, hotlinking or mis-sourcing a protected wordmark, this campaign uses the company name as plain typeset nominative identification rather than a pseudo-logo. This is a specific technical/provenance limitation, not a generalized endorsement concern. No logo binary or proprietary font is copied.

## Color provenance

- `--official-ink` reflects the black identity treatment visible on current public SCIEX surfaces.
- `--source-cyan`, `--source-blue`, `--source-violet` and `--source-silver` are documented source-sampled approximations from current public SCIEX identity, product and software pages. They are not represented as a published official palette.
- `--candidate-aqua` is candidate-original and supports the AI Method Spectrum.

## Typography

No authoritative SCIEX typography standard was located. The campaign uses narrow, technical sans-serif system stacks and does not copy proprietary font files. The decision prioritizes public-site structural character, platform availability and stable PDF output.

## Imagery and visual grammar

No company photography or product render is copied. Employer recognition comes from SCIEX naming, public-source color behavior and an original mass-spectrometry-inspired spectrum. The AI Method Spectrum is candidate-created and serves the role argument; it is not presented as SCIEX product output.

## Artifact application

Website, resume, cover letter, interview brief, 120-day plan and AI Deployment Assay use the same black/white technical foundation, cyan/blue/violet spectral accents, narrow display typography, ruled method surfaces and explicit independent-candidate language.
